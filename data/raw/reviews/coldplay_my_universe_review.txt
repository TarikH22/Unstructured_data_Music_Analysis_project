Author: Alex Turner
Written: 2021-10-15

Coldplay have always had a gift for making the grandiose feel personal, and
"My Universe" is no exception. Collaborating with BTS was a bold move, but it
pays off — the track layers English and Korean vocals seamlessly over a
pulsing synth-pop backdrop.

The lyrics lean into Coldplay's classic themes: love as a force that defies
gravity, distance, and doubt. Nothing groundbreaking, but delivered with
enough sincerity that it lands. Chris Martin's falsetto in the bridge is
quietly spectacular.

Production-wise it is maximalist but disciplined. The drop never feels cheap.
If anything it earns it. A crowd-pleaser that doesn't embarrass itself.

Rating: 8/10
